SUSPECT 1 · THOMAS HOBBES

Hobbes’s file

What did Hobbes fear most?

Thomas Hobbes lived in England during a civil war in the 1640s. A civil war is a war between groups inside the same country. Hobbes watched people fight over who should rule. He decided that the worst thing for people was living in constant fear of violence. He called life with no government the state of nature, a time of endless violence.

Who should have the final say?

In his book Leviathan (1651), Hobbes said people should agree to obey one strong ruler. He called this ruler the sovereign. The sovereign has the final say over the laws. Hobbes called this final say sovereign power, and he said it must stay whole, in one place. If two groups both claimed the final say, they would start a conflict, a fight between sides. People give up nearly all their freedom, he said, to get peace and safety. Once they agree, they must submit to, or obey, the sovereign and may never rebel.
